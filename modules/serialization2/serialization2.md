Serialization2 Module
============

@namespace lagrange::serialization

@defgroup module-serialization2 Serialization2 Module
@brief Binary serialization of meshes and scenes.

Serialize and deserialize SurfaceMesh, SimpleScene, and Scene objects to and
from a compact binary format. The suggested file extensions are `.lgm` for
SurfaceMesh, `.lgs` for SimpleScene, and `.lgx` for Scene.
