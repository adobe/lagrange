Volume Module
============

@namespace lagrange::volume

@defgroup module-volume Volume Module
@brief Volumetric mesh representations.

- Create and persist sparse voxel grids from meshes or point samples: [Grid](@ref lagrange::volume::Grid), [mesh_to_volume](@ref lagrange::volume::mesh_to_volume).
- Sample and process grids with coordinate transforms, resampling, offsets, redistancing, and CSG operations.
- Convert volumetric grids to surface meshes: [volume_to_mesh](@ref lagrange::volume::volume_to_mesh).
